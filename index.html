CORE CAPABILITIES

Competitive Intelligence, Reimagined.

Transform competitor website changes into meaningful business insights with automated monitoring and AI-assisted analysis.

🔍

Intelligent Monitoring

Monitor competitor websites using Axios and Puppeteer to collect information from static and dynamic web pages.

🧠

Semantic Change Detection

Use ONNX sentence embeddings and similarity comparison to identify meaningful changes beyond simple text differences.

📊

AI Impact Analysis

Analyze detected changes with Gemini or a local model, producing summaries, impact scores, and recommended actions.

🤖

MIRA Oracle

Explore market intelligence through an AI strategy co-pilot for competitive positioning, pricing, and SWOT analysis.

⚔️

War Room Simulator

Evaluate hypothetical business moves and generate potential competitor responses, risk assessments, and action plans.

📢

Automated Alerts

Connect intelligence workflows with Slack, email, Notion, and Airtable for notifications and organized records.

🃏

Competitive Battlecards

Generate competitor summaries containing strengths, weaknesses, sales questions, and potential objections.

🧩

Chrome Extension

Register competitors from the browser and configure connections to the MIRA backend.

🔄

Resilient Processing

Use cloud, local-model, and rule-based fallback strategies to handle certain inference failures.

ABOUT MIRA

Understand Competitors. Act Strategically.

MIRA is designed to reduce the manual effort involved in competitor research by combining website monitoring, machine-learning-based change detection, AI analysis, strategic simulation, and workflow integrations in one platform.

🎯

The Goal

Make competitive research more systematic and turn market signals into information that teams can evaluate and act on.

⚡

The Approach

Combine automated data collection, semantic comparison, AI-generated explanations, and notification workflows.

🚀

The Outcome

Help users review competitor movements, explore possible strategies, and organize intelligence for decision-making.
